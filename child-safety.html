Child Safety Standards

Lizzumi (com.lizzumi) · Last updated: October 22, 2026

Lizzumi is a social network for sharing photos and videos of pets. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page describes the standards we apply to keep children safe on our platform.

1. Prohibited content and behavior

The following is strictly prohibited on Lizzumi:

  • Any content that sexually exploits or abuses children, including CSAM in any form (real, drawn, or AI-generated).
  • Grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, or any attempt to contact minors for sexual purposes.
  • Sexualization of minors, including sexualized comments, captions, or messages about children.
  • Sharing, requesting, or linking to content that exploits children, including on other platforms.
  • Trafficking, sextortion, or any other form of child exploitation.

2. Age requirements

Users must meet the minimum age required by the laws of their country to use Lizzumi. Accounts found to be operated by users below the applicable minimum age will be removed.

3. In-app reporting

Users can report any profile or post directly in the app using the Report option, and can block any profile to prevent further interaction. Reports are reviewed by our team, and reports involving child safety are prioritized.

4. Enforcement

When we identify or are notified of content or behavior that violates these standards, we will:

  • Remove the violating content as quickly as possible.
  • Permanently ban the accounts involved.
  • Preserve relevant information as required by law to support investigations.
  • Report confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or the relevant regional and national authorities, in accordance with applicable law.

5. Compliance with law

Lizzumi complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, and cooperates fully with law enforcement in investigations involving the safety of children.

6. Child safety contact

If you have a child safety concern, want to report CSAE content, or are a law enforcement agency, contact our designated child safety point of contact:

If a child is in immediate danger, please contact your local emergency services or law enforcement right away. You can also report child exploitation to NCMEC at report.cybertip.org.
